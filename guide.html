Before you buy a used iPhone

Five quick checks. We are happy for you to do them in front of us.

Check the battery

Open Settings, then Battery, then Battery Health. A higher percentage means more life left.

Make sure it is not locked

The phone should be signed out of iCloud with Find My turned off. A locked phone can be unusable.

Check the serial number

Look up the serial number on Apple’s coverage page, checkcoverage.apple.com, to confirm the model and warranty status.

Test the basics

Try both cameras, Face ID or Touch ID, the speakers and microphone, the charging port, and a SIM card for signal.

Look at the screen and body

Check for dead pixels, burn-in and dents. Ask us how each listing is graded.
